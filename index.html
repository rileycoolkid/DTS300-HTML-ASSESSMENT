A simple guide to better rest

Small habits can lead to better sleep.

Good sleep supports your mood, focus, memory, and overall health. Start with one calming change tonight and build from there.

Simple habits for tonight

  • Keep a regular bedtime and wake-up time, even on weekends.
  • Dim lights and put away bright screens about an hour before bed.
  • Keep your bedroom cool, quiet, and as dark as possible.
  • Avoid caffeine late in the day and heavy meals close to bedtime.
  • Use a relaxing routine such as reading, stretching, or slow breathing.

How much sleep do we need?

Adults
7–9 hours
Teenagers
8–10 hours
School-age children
9–12 hours

Listen to your sleep

Everyone’s needs are different. If you regularly struggle to sleep, feel very tired during the day, or snore loudly, speak with a healthcare professional for advice.
